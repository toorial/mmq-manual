About MMQ

MMQ (Multifunctional Meat Quality Determination Software) measures meat colour, marbling and intramuscular fat from cross-section images. It is written for Windows and is aimed at enterprise users. The default species is pig. A separate browser tool, PorkMS, grades pork marbling from fat area ratio without installing the desktop program.

PorkMS

Online
Marbling

Pork Marbling Measurement System runs in the browser. It reports fat ratio and a marbling grade. Images are processed locally and are not uploaded.
